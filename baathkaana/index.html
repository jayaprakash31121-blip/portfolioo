About

BaathKhaana Foodcourt is a casual, drive‑in style hangout in Miyapur — perfect for friends, tea, snacks, and hearty breakfasts. We blend warm ambiance with top‑notch hygiene and pocket‑friendly prices so you can enjoy great taste every day.
